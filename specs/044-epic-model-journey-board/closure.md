## Constitution XII — execution registration (`T1609`)

The commands that produced this Epic (`/speckit-specify` through `/speckit-implement`) ran
**unregistered by hook**: this repository is not a PMI-managed project, has no `.pmi/project.json`,
and the extension's hooks fire only inside one. The Epic's own executions are therefore recorded
here and in the commit history, not in a registry.

What this Epic changes for the projects that *are* managed: the first run over the composed
application now registers **one `specify` execution per Epic of the entity** and records the
decomposition decision the registry admits (`T1591`, `T1593` — over the test server, where two
and four executions respectively were registered by `speckit.pmi.begin` and completed by
`speckit.pmi.finish`). The M3 transcript (`T1607`) will be the first such registration against a
running stack; until it runs, that claim rests on the composed-application tests, not on a stack.
